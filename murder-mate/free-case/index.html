The rules in one minute

The board is the palace. Every square belongs to a room. The black king is the victim. The white pieces are the suspects, except the white king (you, Inspector Rao) and the white pawns (guards, none tonight).

The killer acts alone. Exactly one white piece moves and gives mate in the number of moves stated. Several suspects can usually manage it. That is the point.

The witnesses. “X never left the Library”: every square X passed through or landed on is in the Library. “Nobody set foot in the Stepwell”: no suspect passed through or landed on it. “Somebody went through the Kitchens”: the killer crossed it or landed there. The square a suspect stood on before the murder never counts, and knights only count their landing square.

Your verdict. Who gave mate, the room where the mating move landed, and how: back-rank mate, smothered mate, queen's kiss (the queen mates from a square touching the king), or a mate named after the piece.

Rookie Mate in one · White to play

The victim: Mr Horace Tibbs, gramophone salesman from Birmingham. Mr Tibbs arrived with the rain and three crates of records. At dinner he gave every guest a free sample, one song each, and promised the rest of the collection to anyone who signed on the dotted line. Nobody signed. By ten he was playing the same waltz for the fourth time. By eleven he was dead in the Durbar Hall, one hand still on the crank.

Black: Mr Tibbs (king), his assistant (bishop), and three crates of records nobody ordered (pawns).

The solution

Four guests can give mate in one. The testimonies clear them one at a time.

Captain Arjun has only Rxa4#, and a4 is in the Durbar Hall, where nobody set foot.

Dr Hale has only Be7#, which never leaves the Mirror Hall. Then nobody would have gone through the Courtyard.

Mr Irani could play Nb5# or Nc2#, but both take him out of the Durbar Hall, and he never left it.

That leaves Lady Devika, who had two ways to do it. Qxa4# crosses the Durbar Hall, so it is ruled out. Qc5# lands in the Monsoon Courtyard. The queen does not touch the king from c5, so it is not a kiss: it is a plain Queen's mate.
